SnackArcade is run by one person, and messages are read by the person who can actually fix things. That is the main advantage of a small site, so please use it.

## Email

The fastest route is email. Use the address that matches your subject so it gets to the right place:

| What it's about | Email |
|---|---|
| General questions, feedback, hello | **hello@snackarcade.com** |
| A broken game or a wrong controls table | **bugs@snackarcade.com** |
| Suggesting a game to add | **games@snackarcade.com** |
| Privacy, data requests, GDPR and CCPA | **privacy@snackarcade.com** |
| Copyright and DMCA notices | **dmca@snackarcade.com** |
| Advertising and business enquiries | **hello@snackarcade.com** |

Expect a reply within a few working days. Data-protection requests are answered within 30 days, as required by law.

## Reporting a broken game

This is the most useful message we receive, and a good report gets fixed far faster than a vague one. Please include:

1. **Which game** — the page URL is ideal.
2. **What happened** — did it fail to load, freeze mid-game, or behave incorrectly?
3. **Your browser and device** — for example "Chrome on Android" or "Safari on an iPhone".
4. **Whether it happens every time** or just once.

If a control listed in a game's controls table does not do what the table says, that counts as a bug and we want to hear about it. Those tables are written by hand and hand-written things contain mistakes.

## Suggesting a game

Suggestions are welcome, with one caveat that saves everyone time: a game can only be added if its licence permits use on an ad-supported site. In practice that means open-source games under MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, ISC, Zlib, CC0 or CC BY licences, or games whose developer gives written permission.

Games under GPL, AGPL, or any Creative Commons licence with a NonCommercial (NC) or NoDerivatives (ND) clause cannot be used here, no matter how good they are. If you know the licence when you write, please mention it.

## If you made a game we host

Every self-hosted game on this site credits its developer by name with a link to the original repository and licence. If your attribution is wrong, incomplete, or you would like the wording changed, email **hello@snackarcade.com** and it will be corrected quickly.

If you would prefer your game not be hosted here at all, that request is honoured without argument — email **dmca@snackarcade.com** or see the [DMCA page](/dmca/) for the formal process.

## What we cannot help with

We cannot recover lost game progress. Scores and save data are stored by your browser on your own device, and we never receive a copy, so once browser data is cleared it is genuinely gone.

We also cannot unblock the site on a school or workplace network. That is controlled by the network administrator, not by us.
