  about:

      is instant, fair and requires nothing but two hands. Its rules are perfectly
      symmetrical — every option beats one other and loses to one other — which is why it
      has become the standard way to settle a tie or make a quick decision.

      picks randomly, so there is no strategy to learn. A smarter opponent follows patterns,
      and beating it means noticing those patterns and choosing the counter — the same skill
      of prediction that makes the game interesting between humans.
