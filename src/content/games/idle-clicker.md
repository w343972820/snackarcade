  about:
    - >-
      Idle games, also called incremental or clicker games, grew from a simple experiment:
      what if a game rewarded you for not playing? The answer is surprisingly compelling. You
      click to earn, buy an upgrade that earns for you, then buy upgrades that multiply the
      upgrades — and before long the game is a numbers puzzle you genuinely enjoy optimising.
    - >-
      Idle Clicker follows the classic arc. Early on, your own clicks are your main income.
      Then upgrades and multipliers take over, and the counter starts climbing on its own.
      Finally, the prestige system lets you reset your progress for a permanent bonus, which
      turns the whole game into a long-term planning puzzle about when to reset.
    - >-
