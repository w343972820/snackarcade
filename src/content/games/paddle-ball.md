  about:
    - >-
      The two-paddle ball game is one of the most influential arcade games ever created,
      launching the home video game industry in the 1970s. Its rules are famously simple:
      keep the ball in play, hit it past your opponent, and be the first to reach the target
      score. Half a century later, it remains the perfect demonstration of how much fun two
      lines and a dot can be.
    - >-
      The game is played on a rectangular court with a paddle on each side. Each player moves
      their paddle up and down to intercept the ball and send it back. The ball's angle
      depends on where it hits the paddle — the edges send it off sharply, the centre sends
      it straight — which is where the real skill lives.

      body: >-
        The ball rebounds at different angles depending on where it hits the paddle. Edge

    - title: "Predict the wall bounces"
      body: >-
        The ball bounces off the top and bottom walls, changing its path to your side. Learn
        to read those wall bounces so you are waiting for the ball where it will arrive,
        not where it is now.
    - title: "Do not overrun the ball"
      body: >-
        Small, precise paddle movements beat big sweeps. Overrunning the ball happens when
        you panic and overshoot; keep your movements tight and controlled.
    - title: "Control the centre of the court"
      body: >-
        A paddle that controls the centre of the court covers the most possible ball paths.
        Let the ball come to you near the middle instead of chasing it to the edges.
    - title: "Vary your shot angles"
      body: >-
        If you always return the ball straight, your opponent will stand still and block
        everything. Mix straight shots with angled ones to keep them moving.
