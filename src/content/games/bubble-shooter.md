  intro: >-
    Bubble Shooter is the matching puzzle that feels like an arcade game: a ceiling full of
    coloured bubbles, a launcher at the bottom, and one simple goal — fire bubbles into the
    cluster to match three or more of the same colour and make them pop. Every pop drops
    the cluster closer to the line at the bottom, and you win by clearing the whole ceiling
    before it reaches you. It is easy to pick up, endlessly replayable, and runs free in
    your browser with no download and no account.

  about:
    - >-
      The bubble-shooting format became a worldwide casual favourite in the late 1990s and
      has never gone away. Its genius is the one-shot-one-result loop: you aim, you fire,
      and the cluster reacts immediately. Every shot is a small decision about angle and
      colour, and the best shots pop several bubbles at once and drop whole chains.
    - >-
      The tension comes from the ceiling dropping toward you. The longer a game lasts, the
      lower the cluster hangs, and the fewer safe shots you have. Skilled players do not
      just match colours — they plan where the popped bubbles will drop, and they use the
      walls to bank shots around the cluster for angles the launcher cannot reach directly.
    - >-
      This version is based on an open-source HTML5 bubble shooter by rembound, released
      under the MIT licence. It runs entirely in your browser with mouse aiming on desktop
      and touch controls on mobile, and offers the same satisfying pop-and-drop loop as the
      classic game. No download, no sign-up, no waiting.

  howToPlay:
    - step: "Aim your shot."

    - step: "Fire a bubble."
      detail: >-
        Click or tap to launch the current bubble into the cluster. The bubble travels until

    - step: "Match three or more."

    - step: "Clear the ceiling."
      detail: >-
        Keep matching and popping until the entire cluster is gone to win the round. If the

    - step: "Bank shots off the walls."
      detail: >-
        The side walls bounce the bubble, letting you reach bubbles that are tucked behind
        others. Angled bank shots are the key to clearing stubborn clusters.

  tips:
    - title: "Aim for the bottom of the cluster"
      body: >-
        Hitting the underside of the cluster pops more bubbles than firing into the middle,
        because the chain reaction drops and breaks a larger area. Shoot low whenever you
        can.
    - title: "Match the wall, not just the cluster"
      body: >-
        A bubble fired at a wall bounces at a clean angle. Use bank shots to reach bubbles
        tucked behind the cluster that a straight shot cannot touch.
    - title: "Clear one colour at a time"
      body: >-
        Working through the colours methodically keeps the cluster tidy and predictable.
        Scattering shots across every colour leaves a messy ceiling with no easy matches.
    - title: "Watch the next bubble"
      body: >-
        The launcher shows the current bubble and often the one after it. Plan two shots
        ahead: if the next bubble is a colour you need high in the cluster, save the current
        shot for somewhere useful.
    - title: "Let hanging bubbles drop"
      body: >-
        When you pop a match that leaves a group of bubbles with no support, the whole group
        falls and scores bonus points. Setting up a big drop is worth far more than a single
        small match.
    - title: "Do not rush as the ceiling drops"
      body: >-
        When the cluster hangs low, every shot matters. Slow down, find the safest match
        that removes the most bubbles, and resist firing at the first angle you see.
