  about:
    - >-
      Idle games, also called incremental or clicker games, grew out of a simple design
      experiment in the early 2010s: what if a game rewarded you for not playing? The
      answer turned out to be surprisingly compelling. You start by clicking, buy something
      that clicks for you, then buy something that buys the thing that clicks for you, and
      before long the game is a spreadsheet you are genuinely excited to optimise.
    - >-
      Idle Venture follows the classic formula. Early game, your own clicks are your main
      income. Mid game, upgrades multiply what each click earns. Late game, managers and
      automated systems earn for you continuously, and the game becomes a planning puzzle
      about which upgrade gives the best return on your next purchase.
    - >-
      This version is based on the open-source game by bitcraft3r, released under the MIT
      licence, and renamed here to avoid confusion with a commercial title of a similar
      name. It runs entirely in your browser, saves your progress in local storage so you
      can return to your growing empire later, and has no purchases of any kind — every
      upgrade is earned by playing.
