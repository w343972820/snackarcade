# Games Like 2048 You Can Play Free

If you have played 2048, you already know the appeal: simple rules, a clean board, and the quiet tension of watching your options shrink as the grid fills up. This collection is for players who love that feeling and want to know what else to play. The good news is that the original is here, free in your browser, with a full guide so you can get the most out of it.

## The Original Is the Best Place to Start

2048 is the sliding tile puzzle that went viral in 2014 and never really left. You slide a four by four grid in four directions, matching tiles merge into doubles, and your goal is to reach the 2048 tile. It is the game that spawned an entire genre of merge puzzles, and the version on this site is the real thing: open source under the MIT license, no download, no sign-up, and your best score saved in your browser.

## What Makes a Good 2048-Like Game

The best games in this family share three features. First, a simple rule set that takes seconds to learn. Second, a strategic layer that emerges from that simplicity — in 2048 it is the corner strategy and the descending row. Third, short sessions with natural stopping points, so you can play for five minutes and walk away without losing progress. The games on SnackArcade are chosen for exactly those qualities.
