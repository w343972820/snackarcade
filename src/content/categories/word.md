Word games are the quietest games on this site and, by a wide margin, the ones people spend the longest on. There is no timer pushing you, no reflex test to fail, and no way to lose by being slow. There is only the gap between the letters you have and the answer you haven't found yet — and the strange fact that staring at that gap for another thirty seconds usually works.

This category covers three things people search for separately. Guessing games, where you narrow down a hidden word from feedback on each attempt. Anagram and letter games, where the challenge is squeezing every valid word out of a fixed set of tiles. And trivia, where the puzzle is recall rather than construction.

Everything here runs in the browser with no download and no account. Games with a daily puzzle reset on a stated schedule, and games without one can be replayed as often as you like — a distinction that matters more than it sounds, because a daily game you have already solved is a page you cannot use again for twenty-four hours.

Each page includes the rules written out properly, the full controls for both physical and on-screen keyboards, and tips that are specific to that game's word list rather than generic advice about vowels. Where a game has a known quirk — a word list that rejects plurals, a hard mode that changes the scoring — it is documented rather than left for you to discover mid-game.

Word games are added regularly. If you are new to the category, a five-letter guessing game is the fastest way in.
