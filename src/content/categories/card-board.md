There's a reason Solitaire has outlived every operating system it ever shipped on. Card and board games have a rhythm to them — deal, consider, commit, repeat — that holds up whether you're playing for two minutes or two hours. This collection brings the classics into the browser, with no download and no card-shuffling animation you can't skip.

You'll find the full Solitaire family here (Klondike, Spider in 1, 2 and 4 suits, and FreeCell), Mahjong Solitaire with multiple tile layouts, plus Chess and Checkers against an adjustable computer opponent. Every game remembers your progress in the browser, so closing the tab doesn't cost you the hand.

If you grew up playing these on a desktop computer, they work exactly the way you remember. If you're learning, each page has a plain-English rules section — no jargon, no assumed knowledge.

What separates a good browser card game from a bad one is usually not the card art. It's whether the undo button works the way you expect, whether the game tells you when no legal moves remain, and whether the deal is genuinely random rather than quietly stacked to keep you playing. Those are the things we check before a game goes on the site, and the things each page tells you about honestly.

Card and board games are consistently the most-played category on this site, and it's the one we add to most often.
