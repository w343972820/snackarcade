Idle games are the strangest genre on the internet and the most quietly compelling. You start by clicking something. Then you buy a thing that clicks for you. Then you buy a thing that buys the thing that clicks for you, and somewhere around the third layer the game stops being about clicking at all and becomes a spreadsheet you are genuinely excited to optimise.

This category collects incremental games that run in a browser tab without demanding your attention. They are the best possible fit for a second monitor, a long download, or a background tab you check every twenty minutes. Sessions in this category are the longest on the site by a wide margin, which is exactly what the genre is designed to do.

The two things that decide whether an idle game is worth your time are both technical, and both documented on every page here: whether the game accrues progress while the tab is closed, and where the save file actually lives. A game that only progresses while open behaves completely differently from one that pays out offline earnings, and a save stored in browser local storage disappears the day you clear your browsing data.

Prestige systems get the same treatment. Knowing when your first reset becomes profitable is the difference between a satisfying run and an hour of grinding that a single well-timed reset would have skipped. Where a game has a recommended first-prestige point, it is written down rather than left to the wiki.

None of these games have purchases, currency packs or paid boosts. Every multiplier in every game here is earned.
